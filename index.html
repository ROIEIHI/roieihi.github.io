About

Device physics, met with data.

I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduate from the Holon Institute of Technology (HIT). My work sits where hardware meets machine learning: designing and simulating silicon-photonic and RF devices, and building the signal- and image-processing pipelines that turn their measurements into results.

That range shows up across my projects — a first-author study using Bayesian optimization to design a photonic modulator, a body-worn antenna simulated in HFSS, a webcam gaze-tracker validated on 187 people, and a critical reproduction of a deepfake detector that exposed where the published method breaks. I care as much about honest evaluation as about a clean result.

01 · PHOTONICS & DEVICES

Silicon-photonic device design

Bayesian Optimization of a PIN Phase Shifter

Objective

Co-optimize a forward-bias PIN modulator's competing metrics — modulation efficiency and optical loss — which the plasma-dispersion effect couples into a nonlinear trade-off that one-factor sweeps cannot solve.

Result

A Gaussian-process surrogate driving Lumerical multi-physics located a valid design in 150 simulations, raising the valid-design rate from 22% to 80% and reaching VπL = 0.649 V·mm at 15.2 dB/cm. A passive RC equalizer then extended bandwidth from 6.25 to 24.9 GHz for 25 Gbps.

02 · RF & ANTENNAS

Antenna & microwave design

915 MHz PIFA Integrated in a Boot

Objective

Design a compact PCB antenna that resonates at 915 MHz while worn on the body, where the foot is a high-loss dielectric that absorbs RF and drags the resonant frequency downward.

Result

A quarter-wave PIFA placed in the rigid heel counter — chosen over the sole and top-of-foot for mechanical robustness and radiation direction — matched to 915 MHz and simulated in HFSS with custom foot, leather, rubber, and concrete dielectrics, giving an off-body directional pattern.

03 · SIGNAL & IMAGE PROCESSING / ML

Pipelines & learning systems

Webcam Gaze Tracking for Reading Analysis

Objective

Turn a standard RGB webcam into a reading-behavior analyzer with no infrared hardware, GPU, or head stabilization — overcoming webcam jitter and the weak vertical-gaze signal.

Result

A CPU-only pipeline — MediaPipe landmarks, engineered cross-term features, a multi-output XGBoost regressor, and a velocity-adaptive Kalman filter — reached 3.6° mean angular error across 187 participants, with reading metrics in the physiological range.

Frequency-Domain Deepfake Detection

Objective

Test whether spectral colour-channel anomalies left by generative upsampling can separate real from fake faces — by reproducing a published method and stress-testing its claims.

Result

The reproduced global spectral method reached only ~65% accuracy on compressed video, while a spatial Xception CNN control hit 81% / 0.93 AUC — exposing the spectral approach's compression fragility and likely frame-level data leakage in the original claims.

Adaptive Wavelet Denoising of Power-Quality Signals

Objective

Preserve the sharp transient edges of a voltage sag while removing additive noise, where a single global wavelet threshold either over-smooths the event or leaves noise behind.

Result

An energy-optimized, scale-adaptive scheme — per-level thresholds from a Peak-to-Sum Ratio plus critical-scale selection — reconstructed the sag and located its start/end via modulus maxima, benchmarked against hard and soft thresholding across the SNR range in MATLAB.
